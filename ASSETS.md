## Consigne de retouche

Use case: compositing. Edit target: supplied MimFlo hero illustration. Primary request: preserve this exact young woman with her face, closed eyes, hairstyle, lavender headphones, lavender shirt, hands and microphone. Add ONE young adult man seated beside her, slightly to the right, in the exact same polished pastel watercolor illustration style, same scale, soft linework, shading, and table. The man has short wavy dark hair, a friendly face, listening or speaking with calm confidence, dusty blue headphones and a navy-blue casual shirt, with a matching dark studio microphone in front of him. Extend the canvas horizontally to comfortably accommodate both people, centered as an equal pair, separated enough that both faces and microphones are clear. Keep soft eucalyptus leaves and peach/lilac backdrop; on the man's side add muted sage and powder blue tones. Transparent outside the illustration, preserve alpha. No text, no typography, no logos or UI. Avoid changing the original woman's identity or style, avoid cropping either person's head or hands, no extra characters. Landscape composition suitable for a French learning website hero.
